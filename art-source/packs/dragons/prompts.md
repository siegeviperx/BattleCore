# Dragon Clans prompts

Each piece was made with `npm run pack:piece -- dragons <slot> --prompt "<prompt>"`. The pack's `style.md` is
added in front of every prompt, and the images in `concept/` are the style references.

| Slot | Piece | Prompt |
|---|---|---|

| q | Storm Queen | a sleek slender teal dragon queen with a glowing pale-blue lightning-bolt crest and a small gold tiara, standing tall on its hind legs, wings half spread, tiny toy sparks around its claws |
| r | Dragon Roost | a chunky round stone watchtower with battlements and a gold pennant flag on top, a small teal dragon coiled around the tower and peeking over the battlements |
